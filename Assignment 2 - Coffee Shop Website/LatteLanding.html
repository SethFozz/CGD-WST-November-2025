Frequently Asked Questions

Is your latte gluten-free?
Yes, our latte is naturally gluten-free, made from coffee and milk ingredients without any gluten additives.
Can I customize the sweetness level?
Absolutely! You can request less or more sweetener at the time of order to suit your taste.
Do you offer alternative milk options?
Yes, we offer oat, almond, and soy milk alternatives for dairy-free preferences.
